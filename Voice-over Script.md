## Steps

1. Produce one audio file per line, using the **file name** given (e.g. `vo_07.mp3`, `safe_03.mp3`).
2. Read the text exactly as written. It is already spelled out for speech (see the pronunciation guide).
3. Keep each process line at or under its **slot**. The **est.** column is the natural read time.
4. Safety lines play while the video is paused, so they have no time limit; aim for 10–15 s each.
5. Export MP3 (or WAV), 44.1 kHz, mono, peak around −3 dB, with no more than 0.2 s of silence at the start or end.
6. Upload the files back into this project (a single .zip is fine). I'll wire each to its cue; the browser voice fills in for any missing line.

## Pronunciation guide

- **T L G O**, **G O**, **D M F**, **B C K**, **S D S**: say each letter.
- **P one hundred**: the product name, P100.
- **P H**: pH, said as letters.
- **Pre G**: "pree gee".
- **Furnace one / furnace two**, **eight fifty** (850 °C), **two point seven five** (2.75 kg): say as written.
- Tone: calm, clear and confident, like a training instructor. Leave a short beat at each full stop and colon.
